❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is YTScript really free?
Yes, completely free. We're supported by advertising, which allows us to keep the service free for everyone.
Do I need to create an account?
No. YTScript works without any registration or login. Just paste a URL and go.
What videos work with YTScript?
YTScript can extract subtitles from any YouTube video that has captions available. This includes auto-generated captions, manually added subtitles, and community-contributed captions. If a video doesn't have captions, we cannot extract them (we don't create transcripts, we retrieve existing ones).
Is it legal to use YTScript?
YTScript retrieves publicly available subtitle data from YouTube. However, you are responsible for how you use the extracted content. Please respect copyright laws and YouTube's Terms of Service.
Do you store my extracted transcripts?
No. We don't store any video URLs or transcripts on our servers. Your recent extraction history is stored locally in your browser, which you can clear at any time.
Why can't I extract subtitles from some videos?
Some videos may not have subtitles available, or the creator may have disabled captions. In these cases, extraction isn't possible.
Can I extract subtitles in different languages?
Yes! If a video has subtitles available in multiple languages, YTScript will show you all available options.
How accurate are the transcripts?
The accuracy depends on the source. Manually created captions are typically very accurate. Auto-generated captions vary in quality depending on audio clarity, accents, and background noise.
Can I use YTScript for commercial purposes?
You can use the tool freely. However, the extracted content belongs to the original creators. If you want to use transcripts commercially, make sure you have the necessary rights and permissions.

📖 The Story Behind YTScript

YTScript started as a personal project. Like many good tools, it was built to scratch an itch.

I was spending hours manually pausing videos, copying text, and piecing together transcripts. There had to be a better way. So I built one.

What started as a simple script became a full-featured tool when I realized others had the same frustration. The positive feedback from early users motivated me to refine the design, add more features, and make it available to everyone.

Today, YTScript helps thousands of people every day work more efficiently with video content. And we're just getting started.

🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

"I can't extract subtitles from a video"

  • Make sure the video has captions available (check if CC icon appears on YouTube)
  • Try refreshing the page and extracting again
  • Some videos have captions disabled by the creator

"The transcript is inaccurate"

  • We retrieve existing subtitles from YouTube; we don't create them
  • Auto-generated captions can have errors
  • Try selecting manually-created subtitles if available

"The website is slow"

  • Try clearing your browser cache
  • Disable browser extensions temporarily
  • Try a different browser

"Export isn't working"

  • Make sure you've successfully extracted a transcript first
  • Check that your browser allows downloads
  • Try a different export format
