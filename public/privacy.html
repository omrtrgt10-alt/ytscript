1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use YTScript, you may provide us with the following information:

  • YouTube Video URLs: When you paste a YouTube video URL to extract subtitles, we process this URL to retrieve the associated transcript. We do not store these URLs permanently on our servers.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us through our contact form or email, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message to respond to your inquiry.
  • Feedback and Communications: Any feedback, comments, or suggestions you voluntarily provide to us.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns:

  • Device Information: Browser type and version, operating system, device type (desktop, mobile, tablet), screen resolution, and language preferences.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, features used, extraction history (stored locally in your browser), and referring websites.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, date stamps, and server logs for security and diagnostic purposes.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. See Section 5 for detailed cookie information.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • YouTube Data: When you request subtitle extraction, we retrieve publicly available transcript data from YouTube's servers. This data belongs to the original content creators and YouTube.
  • Analytics Providers: We receive aggregated analytics data from services like Google Analytics about website traffic and user behavior.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

5.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

5.2 Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Required for basic site functionality (e.g., session management, security) Session
Preference Cookies Remember your settings and preferences (e.g., dark/light mode, language) 1 year
Analytics Cookies Help us understand how visitors interact with our site Up to 2 years
Advertising Cookies Used to deliver relevant ads and measure ad effectiveness Up to 2 years

5.3 Specific Cookies Used

First-Party Cookies (Set by YTScript):

  • theme_preference: Stores your dark/light mode preference
  • recent_extractions: Stores your extraction history locally
  • cookie_consent: Records your cookie consent choice

Third-Party Cookies:

  • Google Analytics: _ga, _gid, _gat - Used for analytics
  • Google AdSense: Various cookies for ad personalization and measurement
  • DoubleClick: IDE, DSID - Used for advertising purposes

5.4 Managing Cookies

You have several options to manage cookies:

Browser Settings:

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete individual or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Clear all cookies when you close the browser

Opt-Out Tools:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative
  • Digital Advertising Alliance
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance

Do Not Track:

We honor Do Not Track (DNT) signals from browsers. When DNT is enabled, we limit tracking to essential functions only.

Note: Blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website and your user experience.
